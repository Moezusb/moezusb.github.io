Release operations with Claude

Castor, clinical trial technology. Product Operations Lead, 2026.

The problem

  • Release notes in regulated software were assembled by hand from Jira, taking anywhere from 15 minutes to over two hours per release.
  • Leadership had no single view of what was shipping, what was at risk, or when freeze dates landed.

What I built

  • A Claude workflow that turns a release's Jira keys into three outputs: a referenced draft, a compliant customer-ready version, and a review checklist for the subject matter expert.
  • A second workflow that prepares live help center articles, removing a Product-to-Support dependency.
  • A permission-aware Release Board, plus sprint and roadmap scorecards for the Chief Product Officer.
  • An analysis of thousands of NPS responses and support tickets (2023 to 2026) that quantified the two biggest pain points.

Result

  • Drafts in under 3 minutes, even across hundreds of Jira keys. No release shipped without live notes.
  • The Release Board was adopted by 26 people and cut weekly planning meetings by half. The feedback analysis was reviewed favourably in a compliance audit.

The controls were the design: every claim traceable to a ticket, every note approved by a named expert.

Intercom Fin AI, end to end

Acuity Insights, B2B SaaS. Senior Operations Associate, 2022 to 2026.

The problem

  • A support team serving enterprise customers was spending its hours on repeat questions that had known, documented answers.

What I built

  • The ROI model and business case that secured executive approval.
  • Requirements, current- and future-state processes, and the evaluation criteria, written before the pilot began.
  • A controlled pilot and acceptance testing, then a production rollout with human-in-the-loop controls and escalation paths.

Result

  • 67% of inquiries resolved autonomously and more than 150 hours of manual work returned to the team each month.
  • Alongside it: escalations redesigned (about 50% fewer), and a 90-day contractor forecast that held 100% SLA adherence for a 20+ person team.

What I learned there became the AI Governance Framework.

Production work

Release operations with Claude

Product Operations Lead at Castor, clinical trial technology, 2026

The problem

Release notes in regulated software were assembled by hand from Jira, taking anywhere from 15 minutes to over two hours per release. Leadership had no single view of what was shipping, what was at risk, or when freeze dates landed.

What I built

  • A Claude workflow that turns a release's Jira keys into a referenced draft, a compliant customer-ready version, and a review checklist for the subject matter expert.
  • A second workflow that prepares live help center articles, removing a Product-to-Support dependency.
  • A permission-aware Release Board, plus sprint and roadmap scorecards for the Chief Product Officer.
  • An analysis of thousands of NPS responses and support tickets from 2023 to 2026 that quantified the two biggest pain points.

The result

Drafts in under three minutes. Every release note published on time, live, and compliant. The Release Board became the shared planning view for Exec, Product, and Engineering, and the feedback analysis was reviewed favourably in a compliance audit.

Intercom Fin AI, from business case to production

Senior Operations Associate at Acuity Insights, B2B SaaS, 2022 to 2026

The problem

A support team serving enterprise customers was spending its hours on repeat questions that already had known, documented answers.

What I built

  • The ROI model and business case that secured executive approval.
  • Requirements, current- and future-state processes, and evaluation criteria, written before the pilot began.
  • A controlled pilot and acceptance testing, then a production rollout with human-in-the-loop controls and escalation paths.
  • Alongside it, redesigned escalation workflows and a contractor forecast that looked 90 days ahead for a 20+ person team.

The result

67% of interactions resolved autonomously, more than 150 hours of capacity freed every month, about 50% fewer escalations, and 100% SLA adherence. What I learned became the AI Governance Framework.
